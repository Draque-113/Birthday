THE BIRTHDAY PARADOX

The **Birthday Paradox** is a fascinating probability puzzle that shows how our intuition about coincidences can be misleading. It asks: *How many people need to be in a room for there to be a greater than 50% chance that at least two of them share the same birthday?* Surprisingly, the answer is only **23 people**. This seems strange because there are 365 possible birthdays, so we might expect hundreds of people to be needed. The reason is that every person can be compared with every other person, creating many possible pairs. With 23 people, there are **253 different pairs**, giving plenty of opportunities for a match. In fact, with 50 people, the probability rises to about **97%**, making the birthday paradox a great example of how probability can behave in ways that seem surprising at first.
