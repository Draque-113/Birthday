DOOMSDAY WAS SCARY

Doomsday was terrifying. The sky had turned an unnatural shade of red, the streets were empty, and a strange silence covered everything. Power had failed across the city, leaving only distant alarms echoing through the darkness. People stood at their windows, watching as enormous clouds gathered above the horizon. No one knew exactly what was happening, but everyone could feel that something was terribly wrong. Phones stopped working, the ground trembled beneath their feet, and the last lights in the city slowly disappeared. For the first time, the world felt completely helpless, as if everything humanity had built could vanish within a single night.
